# Visual Identity: Americans in Alsace (AiA)

## Design Philosophy
A bridge between North American heritage (USA/Canada) and Alsatian residence. The identity is classic, welcoming, and official, mirroring the association's role as a community pillar.

## Color Palette
- **Alsace/Canada Red**: #E30613
- **US Canton Blue**: #3C3B6E
- **White**: #FFFFFF
- **Text Gray**: #111827

## Typography
- **Headings**: Georgia (Serif) - for a timeless, trustworthy feel.
- **Body**: Inter/Sans-serif - for modern readability.

## Logo & Assets
- **Logo**: /public/logo.svg (Recreated with a completed circular frame).
- **Favicon**: Uses the SVG logo directly.

## UI Patterns
- Use **Red** for primary calls to action (CTAs).
- Use **Blue** for secondary elements or navigation backgrounds.
- Maintain generous white space to evoke a clean, professional expat association look.
